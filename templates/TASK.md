# TASK: <bounded outcome>

- Starting point: repository, branch, exact HEAD, expected worktree state.
- Goal: user-visible behavior or resolved problem.
- Read first: current handoff and relevant canonical paths only.
- Change: concrete implementation/documentation delta.
- Invariants: data, ownership, security, behavior and scope to preserve.
- Checks: focused checks, final gates, native/live scope and evidence to reuse.
- Non-goals: adjacent work excluded from this task.
- Done: implementation, reviewed diff, validation, docs, commit and one PR or
  explicitly requested local-only deliverable.
- Authorization: publication, integration and live actions explicitly allowed.
- Resources: applicable runtime/model policy, CI reserve and expensive-run limits.

If a transferable surprise appears, prepare a sanitized case under the adopted
CCS maintenance process. Do not broaden operational scope to investigate it.
